What's next

After running Setup, the next tools to build are: Deliveries (quick goods-in entry — supplier, batch/lot number, best-before date, quantity), a Stock view (real-time availability computed from deliveries minus allocations), Orders (customer orders with stock allocated down to a specific batch), and Picking / Cutting / Packing lists generated from allocated orders. Customer delivery-routing comes later, once customer records exist.
